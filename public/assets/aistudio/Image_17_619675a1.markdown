## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a private wealth mobile banking experience characterized by architectural restraint, disciplined typography, and tangible physical craft. It communicates institutional resilience and understated discretion, deliberately moving away from both hyper-casual neobank playfulness and synthetic, neon-drenched crypto styling.

Drawing from high-end horology and bespoke editorial print, the aesthetic balances modern minimalism with deep, tactile tonal hierarchy. It does not lean on decorative blurs or luminous neon glows; instead, clarity and luxury are achieved through razor-sharp contrast, structural discipline, purposeful micro-interactions, and gold accents calibrated like precision metal inlays against obsidian carbon.

## Colors

The palette operates on a calibrated dark-mode system engineered for OLED screens and extreme visual focus.

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base background: `#151515` (deep obsidian canvas, grounding all views).
  - Surface cards: `#202020` (primary grouping containers, financial ledgers, statement cards).
  - Raised surface: `#292929` (modals, bottom sheets, active sheet headers, elevated toggles).
- **Accents & Precious Metals:**
  - Champagne Gold (`#C8A64D`): Primary brand color reserved for high-conviction calls to action, active navigation pins, and balance hero metrics.
  - Light Gold (`#E4C66A`): Selected states, subtle active outlines, and focused touch points.
  - Deep Gold (`#7A5C10`): Structural containment, secondary badges, and low-priority progress indicators.
- **Typography & Structural Lines:**
  - Primary Text: `#FAF9F5` (warm parchment white ensuring readability without harsh optical glare).
  - Muted Text: `#B8B5AD` (secondary data labels, timestamps, transaction metadata).
  - Architectural Borders: `#3A3832` (precise hairline boundaries and structural separators).
- **Functional States:**
  - Positive / Credit: `#16845B` (calibrated deep emerald for gains, deposits, and settled operations).
  - Attention / Alert: `#A86400` (burnished amber warning state for holds, overdraft cautions, and pending security verification).

## Typography

The typography uses Plus Jakarta Sans across all display, body, and UI tiers to maintain a crisp geometric structure with subtle humanist warmth.

- **Numeric Rendering:** All financial ledgers, account figures, currency sums, and stock quantities must strictly enforce OpenType tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums; font-feature-settings: "tnum" on`). This avoids horizontal jitter when balance figures shift or animate.
- **Hierarchy:**
  - Balance readouts use `display-hero` or `number-display` with tight tracking to anchor financial authority.
  - Transaction descriptions utilize `body-lg` or `body-md` set in Warm White (`#FAF9F5`), while associated timestamps and sub-categories are styled in `body-sm` using Muted Text (`#B8B5AD`).
  - Section headers and uppercase metadata badges employ `label-sm` with expanded tracking (`0.04em`) to establish clear boundaries between functional modules.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for native mobile ergonomics (reference frame: 390x844px), scaling seamlessly between compact hand sizes and large mobile form factors.

- **System Grid & Boundaries:**
  - Horizontal Screen Margin: Fixed at `24px` (`margin`) to guarantee comfortable separation from device bezels and edge gestures.
  - Section Stacking: Uniform `16px` (`space-md`) gap between distinct cards and functional blocks.
  - Column Gutters: `16px` (`gutter`) for side-by-side metric tiles and two-column asset splits.
- **Touch Target Integrity:** Every interactive tap zone adheres to an absolute minimum dimension of 44x44px, with preferred primary buttons and high-frequency actions scaled to 48px or 52px height.
- **Adaptation Rules:**
  - On compact devices (<375px width), balance readouts compress from `display-hero` to `display-hero-mobile`, while outer horizontal margins reduce slightly from 24px to 16px to prevent transaction text clipping.
  - Tablet/Foldable expanded viewports constrain the main banking column to a centered 480px structural shell, preventing financial line items from stretching uncomfortably wide.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through structured tonal layering and precise hairline borders rather than dramatic, soft-focus drop shadows or iridescent halos.

- **The Tonal Staircase:**
  - Layer 0 (Canvas): Base background `#151515`.
  - Layer 1 (Cards & Groups): `#202020` card surfaces resting flush over the canvas.
  - Layer 2 (Raised Sheets & Modals): `#292929` overlays for quick-actions, payment confirmations, and drawer menus.
- **Structural Separation:** Every card, container, and elevated surface is bounded by a uniform 1px solid border in `#3A3832`. This creates sharp, jewelry-box separation between dark layers in all lighting conditions.
- **Ambient Floor Shadow:** When elevated surfaces (e.g., sticky action trays, modal sheets) float above standard content, they cast an ultra-deep, directional ambient shadow: `0px 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`. No colored or gold ambient glow is permitted; elevation remains architectural, crisp, and anchored.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances approachable handling with tailored precision.

- **Container Radius:**
  - Master cards and statement containers utilize a 16px to 24px radius (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`), creating soft pocketing within the 24px outer viewport margin.
  - Interactive controls (action buttons, text inputs, segment selectors) adhere strictly to a 12px radius, ensuring quick tactile affordance without turning into soft pills.
  - Small chips, status indicators, and currency tags employ an 8px radius to keep micro-data crisp and structured.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Champagne Gold (`#C8A64D`) fill, `#151515` bold typography. Height: 52px. Corner radius: 12px. Pressed state dims subtly to `#7A5C10` with zero blur or light blooming.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** `#202020` surface with a 1px `#3A3832` border and `#FAF9F5` typography. On active touch, the border transitions cleanly to `#E4C66A`.

### Cards & Financial Containers
- Constructed on `#202020` with a 1px `#3A3832` perimeter and 20px padding.
- Virtual debit/credit cards feature an obsidian finish, faint hairline grain, debossed gold chip insignia, and masked card numbers rendered with strict tabular monospace metrics.

### Input Fields
- Height: 54px. Base background `#151515` nested within `#202020` surfaces, or `#202020` when set directly against the canvas.
- 1px border in `#3A3832`. Focus state shifts the border to Light Gold (`#E4C66A`) without ambient shadow rings. Placeholder text in `#B8B5AD`.

### Lists & Transaction Rows
- Arranged with 56px minimum row heights. Left-aligned vendor glyph or avatar set in an elevated `#292929` tile (40x40px, 10px radius).
- Transaction title in `#FAF9F5` (`body-md`), category/timestamp below in `#B8B5AD` (`body-sm`).
- Trailing amount aligned right: debits in `#FAF9F5`, deposits and rewards rendered in Emerald (`#16845B`). Divided by hairlines (`#3A3832`) indented past the leading icon.

### Chips & Filters
- Compact 32px height, 8px radius. Inactive: `#202020` background, 1px `#3A3832` border, `#B8B5AD` text.
- Selected: `#292929` background, 1px `#C8A64D` border, `#E4C66A` label.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- 20x20px dimension. Unchecked: 1.5px border `#3A3832`, transparent interior.
- Checked: `#C8A64D` fill with `#151515` checkmark or center pip. Touch targets padded out to 44x44px.

### Keypad & Biometric Trays
- Bottom-docked payment authentication surfaces using `#292929` surface sheets.
- Dial-pad buttons feature 64x64px circular touch areas with light tactile haptic response on trigger, subtle tap feedback changing background to `#3A3832`, and numeric readouts in `#FAF9F5`.
